Belum Preorder, Pesanan iPhone Duo Kabarnya Sudah Naik 30%

Redaksi LapakStore88 · diolah dari Detik Inet

Apple reportedly raised its production orders for the iPhone Duo by 30% just days before the preorder window opens. The figure comes from reports circulating in China’s Securities Times, citing unnamed suppliers in Apple’s supply chain. If accurate, it signals a surge of anticipation among consumers and distributors alike. The move precedes the device’s first official preorder launch on October 16, 2026, across several key markets. For tech enthusiasts, the numbers raise the question: will the foldable phone meet the hype?

Peluncuran iPhone Duo: Latar Belakang

First unveiled in September 2026, the iPhone Duo introduces Apple’s foray into the foldable smartphone arena. Its design mimics a compact tablet when unfolded, offering a larger display while maintaining a pocket‑friendly size. The device relies on flexible OLED panels and a hinge mechanism engineered for durability. Apple has also tailored its operating system to support multitasking across the two screens, promising a seamless user experience. The launch marks a pivotal shift for a company long known for its rigid iPhone lineup.

The 30% bump in orders was reported by the Securities Times, which leans on anonymous supply‑chain insiders. The source suggests that Apple’s optimism about demand has led the company to secure additional components ahead of the preorder wave. Such a surge is unusual for a brand new product line, hinting that the company expects a strong market reception. However, the reports do not disclose how many units are actually earmarked for consumers versus inventory reserves. Until Apple confirms, the figures remain speculative but noteworthy.

Preorder: Tanggal dan Peluncuran Awal

Preorders for the Duo are slated to begin on Friday, October 16, 2026, with the first wave covering select markets. The initial rollout will likely include the United States, Japan, and parts of Europe, reflecting Apple’s traditional launch strategy. Customers will need to place orders through authorized resellers, and the device is expected to ship a few weeks after the preorder window closes. The timing aligns with the company’s typical product cycle, but the foldable format introduces new logistical considerations. For buyers, the date marks the first real opportunity to secure the device.

Pasokan dan Ketersediaan

Despite the order increase, analysts project that total supply of the iPhone Duo for the remainder of 2026 will stay below ten million units. The figure underscores a potential scarcity relative to Apple’s regular iPhone models, which typically exceed twenty million units annually. The limited production run could stem from the complexity of manufacturing flexible displays and the need to refine hinge durability. Consequently, early adopters may face a competitive scramble for the device. Supply constraints could also drive secondary market prices upward.

An uptick in component orders does not automatically translate into higher consumer sales, as Apple may be stockpiling to buffer against production hiccups. The company’s strategy might involve building a buffer inventory to ensure availability once the preorder window opens. Moreover, the 30% increase could reflect a broader push to secure supply chain resilience, given recent global chip shortages. Thus, while the numbers hint at enthusiasm, they should be interpreted with caution. The real test will come once orders are fulfilled and shipments begin.

Persaingan di Pasar Smartphone Lipat

Apple’s entry into the foldable market pits it against seasoned Android players such as Samsung, Huawei, and Honor, who have been refining hinge mechanisms for years. By bringing its proprietary ecosystem to the foldable format, Apple may offer a differentiated user experience, especially for iOS developers. The competition could spur further innovation in battery life, durability, and app optimization for dual‑screen use. Market watchers anticipate that Apple’s brand strength may offset initial hardware challenges. The move signals a broader industry shift toward flexible displays.

For consumers, the Duo promises a versatile tool for multitasking, media consumption, and gaming. Developers will need to adapt their apps to leverage the larger, foldable surface, potentially opening new avenues for productivity tools. The integration with Apple’s existing services—iCloud, Apple Pay, and the App Store—could enhance the foldable experience. However, users must weigh the device’s higher price point and the risk of hinge wear over time. The long‑term viability of foldable phones will hinge on durability and ecosystem support.

While official pricing remains undisclosed, analysts speculate that the Duo could be priced in the $1,200–$1,400 range, aligning with Apple’s premium flagship tier. The device’s advanced hardware, such as the dual‑screen setup and robust battery, could justify the premium. Some observers suggest that Apple might offer multiple storage variants, adding to the price differentiation. The high price could limit early adoption, but the novelty factor may attract early‑bird buyers. Pricing decisions will be announced closer to the preorder launch.

Apple has yet to issue a formal statement confirming the reported order increase, leaving room for speculation. Industry analysts note that supply chain reports can be inflated to manage expectations. Meanwhile, suppliers have been quietly ramping up production of flexible OLED panels to meet the anticipated demand. Retail partners are preparing for a surge in preorder inquiries. The lack of official confirmation means stakeholders must monitor Apple’s announcements for confirmation.

Post‑preorder, the real pulse of demand will surface as shipments begin rolling out. Early adopters will provide feedback on hinge durability, battery performance, and software optimization. If the Duo meets expectations, Apple could accelerate subsequent models and expand its foldable lineup. Conversely, any significant flaws could dampen enthusiasm and stall future releases. The company’s ability to iterate quickly will be critical in maintaining consumer confidence.

As the preorder date draws near, all eyes will focus on Apple’s execution and consumer response. A successful launch could cement Apple’s position in the foldable segment and reshape expectations for future smartphones. Should the Duo struggle, it may prompt a reassessment of Apple’s hardware roadmap. In either scenario, the market will closely observe how Apple balances innovation, quality, and supply chain resilience. For now, the 30% order bump remains a tantalizing hint of what’s to come.
