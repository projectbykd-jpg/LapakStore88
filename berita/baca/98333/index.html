Kejurnas Padel Jadi Bentuk Keseriusan Cabor Kejar Prestasi Bukan Tren

Redaksi LapakStore88 · diolah dari Detik Sport

LokalStore88 Jumat,16 Oktober 2026.

Imagine watching a racket sport that was once considered a niche hobby suddenly commanding the national stage with full provincial representation. That is exactly what happened when the first-ever National Padel Championship (Kejurnas) officially kicked off its closing phases in South Jakarta recently. This event is not just another tournament on the calendar; it is a critical milestone that signals the solidification of padel as a serious competitive discipline in Indonesia. For athletes who have trained for years, this platform validates their hard work and dedication to the sport. It moves the conversation beyond casual play and into the realm of structured, high-stakes competition.

The timeline of this inaugural event spans from October 12th to the 18th, although the official opening ceremony took place on Friday, October 16th. This period provides a comprehensive window for the 20 participating provinces to showcase their best talents. The depth of the field is evident, as qualification rounds were conducted in the previous month to ensure only the strongest teams reach the main event. Such a rigorous selection process underscores the commitment of the organizing bodies to maintain high standards from the very beginning. It sets a precedent for future tournaments that will likely follow this same level of organization.

Strength in Numbers

Among the contenders, East Kalimantan emerges as a heavy favorite, particularly in the men's team category. They have already secured a spot in the quarter-finals, positioning themselves strongly for the semi-finals the following day. Their strategy involves bringing a full squad of 16 athletes, including reserves, to cover all potential scenarios during the tournament. However, the women's team from the same province had to settle for an exit in the round of 16, a setback that highlights the uneven competitive landscape. Despite this, the province's presence is formidable, featuring several players who hold status as part of the national training center (Pelatnas). This blend of local pride and national-level talent makes them a team to watch closely in the remaining matches.

The absence of key figures adds a layer of complexity to the competition, particularly for East Kalimantan's women's team. Novela Putria, a standout player and member of the national team, was unable to participate due to a minor injury. She sustained this issue after returning from the 2026 Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games, where she had just secured a bronze medal. Her priorities have shifted to recovery and preparation for the upcoming national training camp that begins the following Monday. This decision was made to protect her long-term athletic career and ensure she is fully fit for higher-level international competitions. Her participation in the opening ceremony, however, shows her continued support for the growth of the sport within the country.

More Than Just a Trend

Novela emphasizes that this first Kejurnas is vital for legitimizing padel as a performance sport rather than a fleeting trend. She recalls the early days when the community had to pioneer the sport largely on their own, facing skepticism about its future. Today, the collaboration between athletes, provincial governments, and the national federation has created a sustainable ecosystem. The involvement of local communities further strengthens this foundation, ensuring that the sport has a broad base of support. This synergy is what transforms a popular pastime into a viable avenue for national athletic achievement.

Other elite players share this perspective, viewing the championship as a crucial stepping stone toward larger goals like the National Sports Festival (PON). Diaz, a national team athlete representing East Kalimantan, notes that performing well in this initial Kejurnas builds momentum for future target competitions. He addresses the common criticism that padel is driven by fear of missing out (FOMO) among the public. By establishing a formal national championship, the sport demonstrates its structural integrity and long-term potential. This clarity is essential for athletes who need to see a clear path to national recognition and support.

The broader implications of this event extend beyond the immediate results on the court. It signals to sports agencies and sponsors that padel is ready for more substantial investment. The presence of Pelatnas athletes in provincial teams creates a bridge between local development and national programs. This integration helps identify talent that can be nurtured for international arenas in the coming years. For the general public, seeing elite athletes compete in their country adds a new flavor to the sports landscape. It encourages more people to take up the sport, knowing that there is a clear ladder of competition to climb.

Looking Ahead

As the tournament concludes, the legacy it leaves behind is likely to shape the next decade of padel in Indonesia. The successful execution of a multi-provincial event suggests that the infrastructure and administrative capacity are in place for regular national championships. This regularity is key to maintaining high performance levels and ensuring continuous improvement among teams. The stories of athletes like Novela and Diaz illustrate the personal investment required to succeed in this emerging field. Their journeys reflect the broader narrative of a sport finding its identity and respecting its growing popularity.

In conclusion, the 2026 Kejurnas Padel is more than a sporting event; it is a declaration of intent. It proves that Indonesia is serious about developing padel into a powerhouse sport that can contribute to the nation's medal count in the future. The collaboration between various stakeholders has laid a strong foundation, balancing the excitement of the sport with the rigor of competitive athletics. For the athletes involved, this championship is a vote of confidence in their choice of sport. The road ahead looks brighter than ever, with clear benchmarks for success and a community that is increasingly invested in the outcome.
